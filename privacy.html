Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

QuoteForge ("we", "us") is an early-access product. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it. We keep it deliberately short and plain.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address — when you join the early-access waitlist, we collect the email you submit.
  • Basic technical data — like any website, our host may log standard request data (IP address, browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you about QuoteForge early access, onboarding and product updates.
  • To understand demand and improve the product.

We will not sell your information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

3. Where your data goes

Waitlist submissions are processed by our form provider (Formspree) and delivered to our inbox. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policy.

4. Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time, or ask us to delete your information, by emailing hello@alott.me. We'll action deletion requests promptly.

5. Data retention

We keep waitlist emails only as long as needed to run the early-access program. If QuoteForge does not launch or you ask us to remove you, we delete your data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product develops. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@alott.me.
